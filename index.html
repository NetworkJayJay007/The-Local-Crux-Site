Support

The Local Crux helps climbers find local partners with compatible skill, style, and schedule.

Need help, or want to report an issue?

Email us at Help@TheLocalCrux.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I report or block someone?
Open their profile from Discover, Matches, or the Check-Ins list and use the report or block option. Reports go to a real moderator, not an automated filter.

How do I delete my account or my data?
Email Help@TheLocalCrux.com and we'll take care of it. See our Privacy Policy for what's retained and why.

Is this a dating app?
No. The Local Crux exists to match climbing partners, not romantic partners — misuse leads to account suspension.
