Meet the Team

The two people building The Local Crux.

Anya

Founder & Developer

Anya is the founder and developer behind The Local Crux. After one too many solo sessions scrolling through gym rosters hoping to spot a familiar face, she set out to build the app she wished already existed — one focused on finding real climbing partners, not swipes. She's building The Local Crux from the ground up, with an emphasis on safety, community, and keeping it exactly what it says on the label: a place to find someone to climb with.

Jack

Main Moderator

Jack keeps The Local Crux's community safe and welcoming as the app's main moderator, reviewing reports and helping shape the app based on real feedback from testers. When he's not moderating, he's climbing right alongside everyone else — putting the app through its paces from the inside.

Questions for the team? Reach us at Help@TheLocalCrux.com.
