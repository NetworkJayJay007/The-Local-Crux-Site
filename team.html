Founders

Anya (she/her)

Founder & Developer

Anya is the founder and developer behind The Local Crux. She's an IT professional and veteran who's been climbing for over 13 years, since 2013 — sessions at Devils Lake, Red River Gorge, and a big multi-pitch day out in Mazama, WA among them. After one too many solo sessions, and after a few friends reached out about how hard it was to find a climbing-partner app that actually worked, she decided to build the one she wished already existed — not swipes, just climbers. She's building The Local Crux from the ground up, with the community's help, with an emphasis on safety and keeping it exactly what it says on the label: a place to find someone to climb with, so nobody has to climb alone if they don't want to. Her background in cybersecurity shapes how user data is safeguarded across the app. She's married to Jack.

Anya@TheLocalCrux.com

Jack (he/him)

Co-Founder & Moderator

Jack keeps The Local Crux's community safe and welcoming as co-founder and moderator, reviewing reports and helping shape the app based on real feedback from testers — he also digs into bug reports and feedback himself. He's a disabled veteran and queer, and has been belaying since 2024; he only belays, not climbs, so when he's not moderating he's still right there at the gym, putting the app through its paces from the inside. His background in privacy and security heavily influences how user data is handled across the app. Off the app, he's a cat dad and a gaming nerd. He's married to Anya.

Jack@TheLocalCrux.com
