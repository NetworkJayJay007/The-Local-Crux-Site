Founders

Anya (she/her)

Founder & Developer

Anya is the founder and developer behind The Local Crux, with the assistance of her husband, Jack. She's an IT professional and veteran who's been climbing since 2013. She's gone on sessions at Devils Lake, Red River Gorge, and a big multi-pitch out on the Goat Wall in Mazama, WA. After one too many solo sessions, and friends reaching out about how hard it was to find a climbing-partner app that actually worked, she decided to build the one she wished existed. She's building it from the ground up, with the help of her friends and community. She has put an emphasis on safety and strives to keep it exactly as it says on the label: a place to find someone to climb with. Her background in cybersecurity shapes how user data is safeguarded across the app.

Anya@TheLocalCrux.com

Jack (he/him)

Co-Founder & Moderator

Jack keeps The Local Crux's community safe and welcoming as co-founder and moderator, reviewing safety reports, weighing in on tricky moderation calls, and helping shape the app based on real feedback from testers. He also digs into bug reports and feedback, working with Anya to diagnose and fix problems. He's a queer, disabled veteran and has been belaying since 2024. He is a forever belayer, so when he's not moderating he's still right there at the gym, putting the app through its paces from the perspective of someone actually in the hobby. His background in privacy and security heavily influences how user data is handled across the app, from what's collected to who can see it. Off the app, he's a cat dad and a gaming nerd, enjoying married life with Anya.

Jack@TheLocalCrux.com
