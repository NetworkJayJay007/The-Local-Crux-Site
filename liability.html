Last updated August 20, 2026

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · Liability Disclaimer · Community Guidelines

Climbing carries real risk, and The Local Crux connects you with people it has not met. Read this before you use the App to arrange a climb with someone.

1. Inherent Risks of Climbing

Climbing — indoor, outdoor, bouldering, top rope, lead, trad, multi-pitch, alpine, or any other discipline — carries an inherent risk of serious injury or death, including from falls, equipment failure, belay error, rockfall, weather, and terrain. These risks exist regardless of how you found your partner, and no feature of the App reduces or removes them.

2. Assumption of Risk

By using The Local Crux to find or arrange a climb with another user, you voluntarily assume all risk of injury, death, or property damage arising from that climb, to the fullest extent permitted by law. This includes risk arising from the other person's skill level, judgment, equipment, or conduct, whether or not it matched what their profile represented.

3. Profiles and Certifications Are Self-Reported

Climbing grades, styles, experience, climbing role, and gym certifications shown on a profile are entered by that user and are not verified by The Local Crux. Do not treat a listed grade, style, experience area, or certification as a guarantee of actual skill, and do not rely on it in place of your own judgment about whether to climb or belay with someone. Confirm a new partner's belay competency yourself before you rely on them, the same way you would with anyone you had just met.

4. No Background Checks

The Local Crux does not run criminal background checks, verify identity beyond what a sign-in provider confirms, or vet users in any way before they can create a profile. Blocking and reporting exist to respond to behavior after the fact; they are not a substitute for your own judgment about who to meet and where.

5. Meeting in Person

When meeting a partner for the first time: meet at a public gym or trailhead rather than a private location, tell someone else where you are going and who you are meeting, bring your own transportation, and trust your judgment — end an interaction at any point it feels wrong. These are recommendations, not features the App enforces or verifies.

6. Check-Ins Are Not a Safety Guarantee

Gym check-ins log where and when you checked in; they are not monitored in real time by The Local Crux, do not notify anyone automatically, and are not a substitute for telling a specific person your plans directly.

7. Release

To the fullest extent permitted by law, you release The Local Crux, its operators, and anyone acting on their behalf from any and all claims, liability, injury, loss, or damage arising from or related to your use of the App or an in-person interaction with another user, including a climbing-related injury, a belay or equipment failure, or the conduct of another user.

8. No Professional Advice

Nothing in the App constitutes instructional, medical, or safety certification advice. If you are new to a discipline, seek qualified in-person instruction before relying on it.
