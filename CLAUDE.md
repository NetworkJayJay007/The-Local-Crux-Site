(conventions, release process, Supabase, issue and board rules, cloud environment)
live in the private app repo's `CLAUDE.md`: NetworkJayJay007/The-Local-Crux-Flutter.

session does not load it automatically. Site-specific rules are in its
"The-Local-Crux-Site (thelocalcrux.com)" section (no em dashes in site text, explicit
@media breakpoints, legal pages auto-generated from the app repo, whats-new.html
